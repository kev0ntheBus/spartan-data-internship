# Week 3: Open-Ended Project Transition

**Dates:** June 15-21, 2026
**Mode:** more open-ended, project-based, critique-driven

Week 3 introduces more independent work. You will still have structure, but the work starts to look more like real analysis.

## Focus

- open-ended EDA
- project-based analysis
- real critique
- independent problem solving
- clearer written findings

## Expectations

You should begin writing:

- the question you are answering
- what data you inspected
- what cleaning decisions you made
- what evidence supports your findings
- what limitations remain

## Folders

- `projects/`: Week 3 project work
- `challenges/`: focused practice challenges
- `notes/`: concept notes and critique notes
