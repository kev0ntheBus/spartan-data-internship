# Week 4: Statistics and Analysis Quality

**Dates:** June 22-28, 2026  
**Mode:** mostly open-ended with focused review checkpoints

Week 4 adds statistics foundations to the EDA work started in Week 3.

## Weekly Focus

- descriptive statistics
- distributions
- sampling
- variance and standard deviation
- correlation
- uncertainty
- clearer analysis claims

## Expected Deliverables

- statistics concept notes
- one analysis notebook or script draft
- one short written interpretation
- weekly review in `reports/weekly_reviews/`

## Review Questions

- What does the summary statistic actually measure?
- What assumptions are being made?
- What would make the conclusion weaker?
- What should be visualized before making a claim?

## Folders

- `assignments/`: Week 4 practice and analysis work
- `challenges/`: focused statistics drills
- `notes/`: statistics notes and review questions
